      The signal

      This isn't a festival crowd. It's a weekender crowd that got told it might be 10 days.

      Two thirds of the room says probably or maybe. Only one in four is fully committed. When they explain themselves in the feedback field, the same story shows up over and over: they want to be there, but they can't disappear for 11 days.

      The design implication is bigger than "add flex tickets." It reshapes the whole rhythm. Daytime programming only makes sense on the weekends; weekdays have to leave room for remote work and for the international crew to explore Austin while locals are on Zoom.

      Below: what the numbers say, then what to do with them.

      The scheduling constraint

      At least 8 people explicitly can't do full weekday daytime. A soft majority signalled the same thing.

      Named "can't do 10 days" cluster
      • Wendy · "2–3 nights, wellness-focused"
      • Anna · remote work, workshops 5 pm+
      • Phebe · "4–5 days max, Wed–Sun"
      • Steven · "evenings, or post-noon"
      • Lena · earlier starts + ends
      • Anvita · flex ticketing for partial attendance
      • Bruno · "only Thursday–Friday"
      • Jess · "socials ended by 2 am"

      The wellness / remote-work / partial-attendance signal isn't a fringe request. It's the dominant thread in the open feedback field, appearing across skill levels, locations, and attendance intents.

      The response isn't to shorten the event. It's to design the rhythm so that the same person can attend 3 days, or 7 days, or all 11, without penalty. Weekdays hold evening programming. Weekends hold daytime. Days aren't wasted; they're returned to attendees for work, rest, or Austin.

      This also frees the Paris crew (Laurent + Cécile) to do daytime Austin exploration midweek while locals are working. That's a feature, not a compromise.

      What to actually do about it

      Seven decisions the signal is pushing us toward.

      1. Ticket
        Sell a flexible ticket, not "the 11 days." Weekend-only, full-event, and a middle-week pass. Anvita, Phebe, Wendy, Bruno all name this directly: flex is the difference between "probably" and "definitely" for a real chunk of the 32.
      2. Rhythm
        Lock the weekday-evening / weekend-daytime split. Weekdays start at 7 pm, weekends run full day. Communicate it in the announcement so people don't have to ask.
      3. Ratio
        Recruit leads. The follower-heavy socials complaint is universal (Barbara, Rachel, Darlene, Lena). Recruit driving-distance leads from TX + OK ahead of the announcement. Try a switch-hour experiment in at least one social.
      4. Depth
        Book one workshop that is only concepts. Not moves with a topic label. Sanchita, Lia, Ini, Kenn are all asking for the same thing. Musicality-as-technique or axis-control-as-technique is the pitch.
      5. Tarraxo
        Add a sister-style session. Tarraxo, semba, or fusion. Five people named it unprompted, an underserved appetite in the North American Kiz circuit.
      6. JnJ
        Program one Jack & Jill on a weekend. 18 people want it. Luke wants it serious. Put it on Friday Nov 6 as an anchor for the closing weekend.
      7. Community
        Design non-dance touchpoints on purpose. Board games, a brunch, a hike, a Halloween theme. 52% want experiences programming. It's what turns a festival into an exchange.
      Decide + move

      Lock the rhythm.
      Then announce the tickets.

      The waitlist has given us enough signal to shape the 11 days without guessing. The next unblock is a decision on the ticketing tiers and a Nov schedule the announcement can point to.
