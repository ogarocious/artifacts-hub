      The data shows that people leave, not why. We already dance with everyone and give tips at the practica, and people still don't return. Our best guess is structural: one all-levels class can't be beginner-friendly and still challenge the regulars. That's a hypothesis to test with the one-timer replies, the Sundays survey, and the Level 1 trial, not a known cause.

Moving the second-visit rate from August's 9% back to 35% (May was 47%) roughly quadruples the people entering the part of the funnel where workshops and privates happen.

      The new Sunday

      Level 2 first, then Level 1 with helpers, then everyone into the practica. Pending Esquina's OK on the longer room booking.

      5:30–6:30

      Level 2

      Regulars. The month's arc at Level 2 depth. Same start time as today.

      6:30–7:30

      Level 1 + helpers

      Beginners arrive. Level 2 dancers stay free as helpers, rotate partners, and balance leads and follows.

      7:30–9:00

      Practica

      Everyone. Beginners end their class straight into the social, dancing with people who know what they're doing.

      Why this order

      Beginners never leave before the practica. That is where belonging happens.

      No blind leading the blind. New dancers partner with experienced ones, so they level up faster.

      Helpers get value too: a free review of the basics, and Crew Points.

      Watch: 3.5 hours is a long Sunday for helpers. Aim for 4 to 6 reliable ones, not everyone.

      A path beginners can see

      Cost check. 8 points ≈ 30 min of Charles's time (~$48 at the $95 rate), so 1 point ≈ $6. A workshop pass at 1 point gives back ~15% of a $40 ticket; a private package at 2 points gives back ~3% of $360. That's why purchases earn less than helping and referrals. With 4 helpers, rewards cost about 1 hour a month.

      Perks to decide

      Four kinds of perks, kept separate on purpose. We go through them one at a time; nothing below the agreed lines is locked.

      1. Helping out agreed

      1 Crew Point per Sunday helping in Level 1 (max 1 per Sunday). Helpers also attend Level 1 free. 8 points = a private credit.

      2. Bringing people agreed

      1 point when someone new comes, +1 when they come back. Weighs the same as helping. The return bonus filters out visitors on its own.

      3. Showing up (attendance milestones) open

      How we track it

      Esquina's report arrives a month late and only covers Sundays. Points and follow-up need same-day data.

      Now: sign-in at the door

      A QR form or sheet: name, contact, "who brought you?", helper today?

      Doubles as the first-timer contact list, so follow-up happens within 48 hours, not a month later.

      Next: WhereCanWeDance check-in

      Students scan a Kiz ATX QR and check in on WCWD (they still pay Esquina). WCWD already has ticket check-in codes to build on.

      MVP: QR check-in, referral attribution, organizer-marked helpers, points history, organizer-approved rewards. Workshop and private purchases add points automatically later.

      Done = 4 Sundays of check-ins captured and points awarded with no spreadsheet. Useful to other organizers once it works for Kiz ATX.

      Questions we can't answer from the data yet
      • Why
        Why do first-timers not come back? Level, timing, price, distance, partner nerves, or just life. The one-timer replies answer this.
      • Esquina
        Do our students take other Esquina classes? Passes work studio-wide, so "stopped coming" may mean "switched to tango." Ask Michelle.
      • Format
        Does a 3.5-hour Sunday cost us Level 2 turnout? Watch Level 2 headcount for the first month.
      • Points
        Do points change behavior, or just reward people who'd help anyway? Count referrals and redemptions after 2 months.
      • Who
        How many free intros are locals vs visitors? Add "Do you live in Austin?" to the sign-in.
