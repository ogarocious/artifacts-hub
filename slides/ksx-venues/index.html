      Pull this up on site

      What to ask, in order.

      Ask first · any one of these can end it
      1. 1
        Is Saturday October 31 open?
      2. 2
        How late can the music run? Not the building. The music.
      3. 3
        What is the biggest event you have run in here, and what did it look like? Then: does the patio open up alongside, and does that count as extra space or extra cost?
      4. 4
        What is the floor surface? No portable floor is going down, so whatever is there has to work.
      Then money
      1. 5
        How does the rate scale to a full buyout of the whole space?
      2. 6
        Is there a multi-date rate across several nights?
      3. 7
        Does the minimum absorb load-in and load-out, or can setup be billed separately?
      4. 8
        Can we leave the room set up between dates? Nobody has answered this anywhere. It is worth more than a lower hourly rate.
      Ghostline only · Aug 19
      1. 9
        What does it cost to cook in the commercial kitchen alongside an event?
      2. 10
        Can we bring our own cooks, or does that go through your members?
      3. 11
        Alcohol: bring our own, a fee, or service through you?
      4. 12
        Do the TV monitors drive from a laptop? Brochure confirms sound, headset and 2 wireless mics are included.
      5. 13
        Patio surface, and is it covered end to end?
      6. 14
        Restrooms. The brochure lists two. How have bigger events handled that?
      7. 15
        What insurance do you require, and what are your cancellation terms?
      Check yourself · don't ask
      • ·
        Do one pivot on the ball of your foot. You will know in two seconds.
      • ·
        Count the bathrooms and picture 125 people.
      • ·
        Walk the load-in path from where a van would park.
      • ·
        Find the power and where sound would live.

        Open-status conflict. Carl says they just opened. whatoncewas.org says the doors open Oct 18 with a grand opening. A formal October launch means E4's attention is consumed exactly when we need it.

      The day-rate argument

      ACC rents a 4,000 sq ft room, capacity 216, for $600 a full day. ART at $275/hr charges $3,850 for the same 14-hour Saturday. That gap is the argument for demanding a day rate from ART and dadaLab on Nov 7 and Nov 8, worth roughly $1,000 to $2,800.

      No bar, and what that is worth

Guests bringing their own is the venue’s call, not ours, so outside-alcohol fees are still a live question, not a saving. The frame holds: a grant-funded cultural exchange with no bar. Lead with it.
