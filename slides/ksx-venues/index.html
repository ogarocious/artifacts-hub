      Confirmed by Kyle Evans, Aug 18
      • Dates: Nov 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8 only
      • Saturdays: none available
      • Capacity: 150
      • Curfew: midnight
      • Sound + projection: free on multi-day

      • Catering: outside food fine

      The read

      Book direct, not Peerspace. The Peerspace listing showed $215/hr plus a $185 sound fee. Booking multi-day direct makes sound and projection free. Kyle said so himself.

      Rate is still unquoted. He wants a schedule first, so the number is negotiable and depends on hours, headcount and which days.

      Midnight may be soft. Asked Aug 18 whether it is a lease or ordinance limit versus house preference. That phrasing invites an explanation rather than a no.

      Almost Real Things · reply due tonight

      Rate card · from their own inquiry form
      • Rate: $150 to $400/hr by event type
      • Minimum: 4 hours
      • Hours: 24/7, no curfew
      • Capacity: 200 inside, 600 whole site
      • Space: 1,929 sq ft gallery + 1,600 sq ft atrium
      • Included: 3 tables, 30 chairs, 1 Bluetooth speaker
      • Setup: counts inside your rental hours
      • Cleanup: you do it, to their checklist
      The read

      The rate lever is event type. A 125-person ticketed party running late with alcohol prices toward $400. A grant-funded international cultural exchange hosted in an arts complex prices toward $150. Both are true. Lead with the second.

      No curfew is the single biggest advantage over dadaLab's midnight.

      Slow comms remain the risk. Two days of silence broke only after an email plus an Instagram nudge on the same day.

      Email sent Aug 18, 1:50pm tori@almostrealthings.com Halloween conflict unconfirmed Concrete floor
