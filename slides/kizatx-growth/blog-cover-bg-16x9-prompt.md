# Kiz ATX blog cover — 16:9 background only

**Format:** 1536×864 (16:9 landscape). Background composition only. No text. No people. No graphic overlays.

## Creative direction

Match the visual language of the Kiz ATX "Room Doubled" hero (blog-cover.png) exactly, minus the type: cinematic navy atmosphere with soft electric-blue radial glows and the Austin downtown skyline over Lady Bird Lake as a subtle cinematic band across the bottom, shot from the Zilker / Lou Neff vantage in blue-hour grade. This backdrop will be reused across multiple blog covers, so it needs to feel atmospheric and open — the entire upper two-thirds should be a calm navy field ready for typography overlay.

## Palette (strict)

- Background navy: `#0d1520`
- Midnight deep: `#050914`
- Electric blue accent: `#4fc3f7` (subtle rim glow on skyline, atmospheric radial glows)
- Sparkle white: `#dcefaa` (distant city and star sparkle only)
- No gold, no orange, no purple, no green, no rose

## Composition

**Full frame — top two-thirds:**
Open atmospheric navy field. Deep navy `#0d1520` base with two soft radial-gradient glows — one in the top-left corner and one in the bottom-right corner, each in electric blue at ~10 percent opacity fading to transparent. Visually calm and ready to hold large typography. Subtle vertical gradation from deeper navy at the corners to slightly lifted electric-blue-tinted mid-frame.

**Bottom band (~25–30 percent of frame height, spanning full width):**
A cinematic aerial photograph of the Austin downtown skyline over Lady Bird Lake, shot from the Zilker / Lou Neff vantage looking northeast. Blue-hour grade — the sky transitions from deep midnight above into a soft blue-grey glow just above the skyline. Illuminated windows in the towers read as small warm points, low-contrast. The photograph blends seamlessly upward into the navy field with no hard horizon line, feathered gently. Water surface at the base catches a soft electric-blue reflection. Above the top edge of the skyline silhouette, a subtle electric-blue rim glow suggests the city is exhaling light into the sky.

**Global atmosphere:**
- Very fine film grain overlay (~5 percent opacity)
- Subtle scatter of tiny sparkle-white pinpoints in the upper sky, like distant stars or city lights, very restrained
- Overall mood: cinematic, editorial, restrained, magnetic. Deep. Quiet. Premium.

## Constraints (non-negotiable)

- BACKGROUND ONLY. Do not render any text, words, letters, numbers, characters, logos, watermarks, brand marks, or graphic overlays. Pure photorealistic atmospheric environment only.
- No people. No dancers. No portraits. No silhouettes of figures.
- No dance-related iconography (no dance floors, no disco balls, no spotlights, no shoes, no music notes).
- No borders, no frames, no decorative elements.
- No warm colors — no gold, no yellow, no orange, no red. This is a strictly cool navy plus electric blue palette.
- No hard horizon lines — the skyline must feather into the atmospheric field softly.
- No neon effects — glows must be subtle and atmospheric, not glaring.
- Composition must feel symmetric and balanced across the horizontal axis, since typography will be placed in various positions on top.
