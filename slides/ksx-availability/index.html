      Read the grid

      What the grid decides, and what it does not

      Settled

      Factory is cheaper and covers more

      $8,000 all in, no tax, for all nine nights, plus the venue from 9am every day. RichesArt quoted $9,038.88 for eight nights, evenings only, tax included, with a $500 deposit on top. Adding Nov 5 and the two daytime blocks pushes RichesArt past $10,000.

      The real question

      Nobody has a verified floor measurement

      Factory's ~1,700 sq ft cannot be traced to a measurement and sits exactly on the bottom of our own brief, which usually means it was assumed. RichesArt now has three conflicting figures: 3,500 in the proposal, 2,550 on Peerspace, about 1,050 measured off their own floor plan. Ask both for dimensions.

      Where to push

      Cabana went backwards and is now out

      Jana retracted Nov 2 on 7 Sept: a third party holds the Clubhouse every Monday 7 to 9pm and Wednesday 8pm to 1am, which also explains Nov 4. That drops Cabana to 6 of 9. Her comparable-day beverage minimum is $5k to $15k, on a flat rate plus tax and service fee. Out of range, and a revenue share was ruled out.

      Not signed

      Contract is the only thing left

      Kara needs the event hours, a name and an email to draft it, and a 50% deposit holds the dates with the balance due the day before Oct 31. Until that is signed, RichesArt stays warm as the fallback. The one thing that could still overturn this is physical: columns, ceiling height, sightlines or a floor that does not hold up across nine nights. Walk the room again before the deposit clears.
