      Read the grid

      What the grid decides, and what it does not

      Solved

      Every night now has at least two options

      The holes do not overlap. Cabana's two refusals, Nov 4 and Nov 7, are both covered by Factory and RichesArt. Factory's one booking inside the run, Nov 5, is covered by Cabana and RichesArt. Sat Nov 7 was the biggest open risk in this search and it now has two venues behind it.

      The real question

      RichesArt just lost the argument that was carrying it

      Its one unique claim was that it alone could take the entire run, and that claim was carrying its size problem. Factory now matches it on coverage and beats it on floor: about 1,700 sq ft against about 1,050. Factory is the only one of the three that meets the venue-brief.md bar.

      Where to push

      Six of nine nights are open at all three

      Nov 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and 8 have every venue available, so quotes compete across most of the run. Only Nov 4 and Nov 7 are down to two, both because Cabana does not offer them. Oct 31 makes seven if Daniela's conditional turns into a yes.

      Blocking everything

      Not one venue has given a price

      Cabana wants top choice dates before quoting and has not replied since 4 Sept. RichesArt's proposal, built around the $7,000 figure, has not arrived. Factory's $200/hr would be roughly $10,900 across the 54.5 programming hours, about $3,900 more than RichesArt, but that rate has never been confirmed for this booking. The size advantage may be exactly what you are paying for.

      • Factory on 5th6 Sept, by text
